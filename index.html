What it can do

Everything in one friendly controller

Built for classrooms, labs and expo stalls. No coding needed on the day.

Talk naturally

Tap the mic and ask. It replies with a clear Indian English voice and every word scrolls on its LCD at the same time.

Its own brain

Knows CognoSpace, Vignan School and lots of science. Add a Gemini key for even smarter answers, if you want.

Drive and gesture

Joystick driving, wave, clap, dance, nod, grab and give. Or just say "go forward" or "raise your right hand".

Expressive eyes

Ten OLED moods: happy, surprised, wink, love, sleepy and more, all mirrored live on screen.

Music and games

Five buzzer tunes and an LCD runner game with three levels. Jump with a tap or by voice.

Full servo control

Head, both shoulders and both elbows, 0 to 180 degrees, with quick poses.
